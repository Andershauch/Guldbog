# Guldbog

**Know what every item on every character is worth – and what to do with it.**

Your bags are full, your bank is full, and your alts are hoarding things you forgot about. Guldbog collects the bags, bank and mail of all your characters in one list and gives every item a clear suggestion: use it, learn it, keep it, sell it on the Auction House, sell it to a vendor, or get rid of it.

## Sell smarter

[BILLEDE: kort-1-inventory.png]

- **AH or vendor?** Guldbog compares the AH price with the vendor price and shows the real profit after the AH cut and the deposit. You see at once where each item sells best.
- **Worth nothing?** When the deposit costs more than the AH pays and no vendor will buy it, Guldbog tells you. Keep it or destroy it, and free up the space.
- **Already on the AH?** Items you have up for auction get their own group, so you don't post them twice.
- **One click to sell.** At a vendor, click a "Sell to vendor" line and every stack of that item in your bags is sold. At the AH, click a "Sell on AH" line and the item opens in Auctionator's Sell tab with the current price, ready to post.

## Craft or sell your materials?

[BILLEDE: kort-2-crafting.png]

Ore, cloth, herbs and leather can go straight to the AH, or they can become something worth more. Guldbog works it out for your characters and their professions:

- **Profitable recipes per character.** Every recipe your characters know is listed with the sale price, the cost of materials and the profit per craft.
- **Fair material prices.** Materials are priced at what you could have sold them for, so gathered materials are not counted as free.
- **Materials from all characters.** "Can make" counts what is in bags, banks and mail across your characters.
- **Use it yourself.** If one of your characters has a recipe that uses an item and still gives skill, Guldbog suggests keeping it for that character. Recipes are shown with their skill color, so a small loss on an orange recipe can still be worth it.
- **Learn it.** Recipes one of your characters can learn are marked for that character.

## Gear for your friends

[BILLEDE: kort-3-friends.png]

Guldbog shows the gear on your characters that someone on your friends list can wear, based on their class and level. Online friends come first.

At a mailbox it takes two clicks: click the item and the letter is ready with your friend's name and the item attached. Then click Send.


## Getting started

1. Install **Auctionator** or **TradeSkillMaster**. Guldbog reads AH prices from Auctionator and uses TSM as a backup. One-click selling at the AH works best with Auctionator.
2. Log in on each of your characters once, and visit the bank on each of them.
3. Open each profession window once (Mining, Tailoring, Cooking, First Aid and so on), so Guldbog knows your recipes.
4. Do a full scan at the AH with Auctionator.
5. Click the coin on the minimap, or type `/guldbog`.

Hover over an item to see which characters have it. Click a column header to sort. Alt+click an item to always keep it.

## Commands and settings

[BILLEDE: gallery/11-settings.png]

- `/guldbog` – open or close the window
- `/guldbog icon` – show or hide the minimap button
- `/guldbog language en` or `/guldbog language da` – English or Danish

Under **Settings** you can change the minimum deposit, the auction duration, the AH cut (5 % faction, 15 % neutral) and whether the deposit is counted as lost.

## Good to know

- The game only shares your bank and mail while they are open. Guldbog remembers what it saw and shows how old the data is.
- Soulbound items are not shown.
- Profit does not include your time.
- "Can wear" means a friend can equip the item, not that it is an upgrade.
