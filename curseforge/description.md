## Lang beskrivelse

**Guldbog** (Danish for "gold book") helps you earn steady gold in WoW Forever. It collects what all your characters have in bags, bank and mail, looks up prices, and tells you what to do with each item.

### Inventory
Everything you own across your characters, grouped by suggestion:
- **Learn / Use** – a recipe one of your characters can learn, or a material one of your recipes still needs.
- **Sell on AH** – you earn more on the AH than from a vendor, after the AH cut and deposit.
- **Sell to vendor** – the vendor pays more, or there's no AH price.
- **Keep** – profession tools, and anything you mark yourself with Alt+click.

Click a row at the auction house to open it in the Sell tab, or at a vendor to sell it. You always press Post yourself.

### Crafting
Every recipe your characters know, with material cost, sale price, profit and skill colour – one group per character.

### Friends
Gear from your bags that someone on your friends list can wear. At a mailbox, one click prepares the mail. You press Send yourself.

### Good to know
- Requires **Auctionator** or **TradeSkillMaster** for AH prices.
- Guldbog **never buys, sells or posts by itself**. Every action needs your click.
- English by default. Type `/guldbog language da` for Danish.
- Open with the coin button by the minimap or `/guldbog`.
- First time: log in on each character and open the bank and your profession windows once.

### Beta
Guldbog is new and tested on a few characters. Class/armour rules are based on Classic and may not match Forever exactly. Please report problems in the comments – a screenshot helps a lot.
