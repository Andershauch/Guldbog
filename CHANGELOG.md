# Changelog

## 0.17.2 – 2026-11-22
- The window reopens where you left it, and can't be dragged off the screen.
- The title shows which character you are playing.
- Sell advice for an item held by another character now says who has it.

## 0.17.1 – 2026-11-20
- Settings: hide a character, so its items and professions are left out of every list.
- The text at the bottom no longer runs under the buttons.
- A character no longer shows up twice after moving to another shard; its bank and recipes are kept.

## 0.17.0 – 2026-11-20
- Guldbog icon in the AddOns list and on the minimap button.
- First release on CurseForge.

## 0.16.1 – 2026-11-19
- Remembers the name of any action the game blocks, to help find the cause.

## 0.16.0 – 2026-11-14
- Collapsible groups: Inventory by suggestion, Crafting by character, Friends by friend.

## 0.15.0 – 2026-11-13
- Click-to-sell on the AH falls back to the game's own Sell tab when Auctionator is missing or has changed.

## 0.14.0 – 2026-11-13
- Settings: language, minimum deposit, auction duration, AH cut, deposit as lost, minimap button.

## 0.13.0 – 2026-11-13
- English text, with Danish as an option (`/guldbog language da`).

## 0.12.x – 2026-11-09 – 2026-11-10
- Friends tab (including Battle.net friends), Forever surnames, browse-scan age in the status line.

## 0.1 – 0.11 – 2026-11-09
- Inventory across characters with selling suggestions, profit after AH cut and deposit, crafting profit list,
  mail and own auctions, click to sell, sorting, status line, TSM as price fallback, secondary professions.
