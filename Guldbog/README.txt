Guldbog – quick guide

Guldbog collects what your characters have in bags, bank and mail, and suggests for each item whether to use it yourself, learn it, sell it on the AH, sell it to a vendor or keep it.

INSTALLATION
- Unzip into World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\ (on Mac: /Applications/World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/). The Guldbog folder must sit directly there with Guldbog.toc inside, not Guldbog\Guldbog\...
- You need Auctionator or TradeSkillMaster. Without one of them there are no AH prices.
- If it shows as "out of date" in the AddOns list, tick "Load out of date AddOns".

FIRST TIME
- Guldbog starts in English. Type /guldbog language da for Danish.
- Log in on each character and open the bank and each profession window once (Cooking and First Aid too).
- Do a full scan in Auctionator at the auction house. Tip: turn on "Run autoscan when Auction House is opened" in Auctionator's options.
- Open Guldbog with the coin button by the minimap or /guldbog.

THE THREE TABS (bottom of the window)
The lists are split into groups. Click a heading to fold it open or closed. Guldbog remembers what you've opened.

- Inventory: everything you own, in the groups Learn / Use, Sell on AH, Sell to vendor, Keep / Tools, Keep / Your choice, On the AH and No profit / No price. The amount next to a heading is what the group can be sold for. "Profit" is how much more you get on the AH than from a vendor, after the AH cut and deposit.
- Crafting: your recipes, one group per character, with material cost, sale price, profit and skill colour.
- Friends: gear that someone on your friends list can wear, one group per friend. Battle.net friends only show while they're logged in.

CLICKING
- A column header: sort (click again to reverse).
- A row at the AH: "Sell on AH" items open in the Sell tab.
- A row at a vendor: "Sell to vendor" items are sold. Undo it from the vendor's Buyback tab.
- A row in Friends at a mailbox: the mail is prepared.
- You always press Post or Send yourself.
- Alt+click (Option+click on Mac): keep the item (e.g. arrows, food, potions) so it isn't suggested for sale. Alt+click again to undo.

SETTINGS
The Settings button at the top of the window: language, minimum deposit, auction duration, AH cut (5 % faction, 15 % neutral), whether the deposit counts as lost, and whether the minimap button shows.

STATUS LINE
The bottom line shows how old the data is. "never" means Guldbog hasn't seen the bank or mailbox on that character yet.

The suggestions are a good guess based on the prices Guldbog knows. Check in Auctionator before selling anything expensive.
